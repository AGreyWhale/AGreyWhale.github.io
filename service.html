North Texas Food Bank

During the summer, I volunteer regularly at the North Texas Food Bank, typically at least once a week. This experience has given me the opportunity to support my local community, develop teamwork skills in fast-paced environments, and contribute to addressing food insecurity. Big Event

Each spring, I participate in The Big Event, the largest one-day, student-run service project in the nation. This annual tradition unites tens of thousands of Texas A&M students to express gratitude to the Bryan-College Station community through various service projects, such as yard work, window washing, and painting. By engaging in these activities, I contribute to fostering a culture of service and appreciation, strengthening the bond between the university and its surrounding community.
