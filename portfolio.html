VR Stroke Rehabilitation Research

As part of the LIVE Lab at Texas A&M, I am contributing to the development of a VR-based rehabilitation environment designed to support stroke recovery. The system guides patients through tailored exercises, tracks their progress, and provides therapists with meaningful data to adapt treatment plans.
My contributions include building an AI assistant powered by Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to deliver medically accurate information with a reduced risk of hallucination. I am also working on integrating Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs) with motion-capture technology to enable precise tracking of patient movements. This dual approach ensures both accurate progress monitoring and a more engaging, effective recovery experience.
Skills used: C#, Unreal Engine, AI/ML (RAG), VR Development, Motion Tracking, Human-Centered Computing

VRTEX Project

Through the LIVE Lab at Texas A&M, I supported a project that uses VR-based esports competitions to make math education more engaging and accessible, particularly for bilingual, rural, and first-generation students.
My contributions centered on the research and organizational side of the project. I wrote sections of the Institutional Review Board (IRB) proposal, helped prepare budget proposals to secure funding, and connected with schools in different regions to explore opportunities for collaboration. By bridging the gap between technical development and real-world implementation, I helped ensure that our product could reach and positively impact a wide range of student communities.
Skills used: Research Design, Grant & Budget Writing, IRB Processes, Outreach & Collaboration

Aggies Lab

At the AGGIES Lab at Texas A&M University, I have conducted research on the impact of AI in social media, with a particular focus on addressing cybercrimes and examining gender dynamics. I published and presented this work at the European Conference on Social Media (ECSM 2025), showcasing the lab's contributions to understanding AI's societal implications. In addition, I led the human-subjects research component, guiding team members through the Institutional Review Board (IRB) process to ensure all research adhered to ethical standards. My responsibilities also included study design, data collection, and analysis, as well as coordinating team workflows to support efficient and high-quality research outcomes.
